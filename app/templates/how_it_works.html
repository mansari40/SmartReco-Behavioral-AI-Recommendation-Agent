The agent pipeline
Observe→ Understand→ Decide→ Retrieve→ Evaluate→ Recommend→ Validate→ Store→ Adapt
The workflow

Four steps, zero guessing

Step 01

Observe everything you do

Every page view, product view, search, click, and second of time spent is captured in batched, non-blocking events. Nothing you do is too small to matter.

Step 02

Understand what it means

The agent analyzes recency, frequency, search intent, and category exploration to build a live picture of your interests, and how they're shifting.

Step 03

Decide when to recommend

It never calls the model after every event. Thresholds, caching, and deduplication gate regeneration, a new topic or a meaningful change in intent triggers a fresh recommendation.

Step 04

Retrieve, validate, deliver

Semantic retrieval over the real catalog finds candidates, which are validated against the SQL database before a personalized recommendation is written and stored for you.

The efficiency contract

What you can rely on

No model call per event

Recommendations are regenerated only when meaningful triggers fire, never on every click.

Catalog-grounded

Every recommendation comes from the real catalog. Products, prices, and IDs are validated against SQL before display.

Non-blocking tracking

Behavioral events are batched and flushed asynchronously, so tracking never slows your browsing.

Reuse over recompute

Recommendations persist and are reused until your behavior meaningfully changes, then they adapt.

Explainable

Every recommendation explains itself in plain, user-safe language based on your observable behavior.

Evolving with you

Explore Python and ML, then shift to RAG and agents, your next step follows what you actually do.
